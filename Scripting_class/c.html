    Hostel Rules and Regulations

    1. Central hostel allotment committee will allot hostels to a particular group of students then room allotment committee at hostel level will allot the rooms to the individual students.
    2. After getting admission in the Institute, a student should report to the office of the Hostel assigned to him/her.
    3. On production of the receipt of payment (Institute and Hostel) temporary allotment of a seat will be made to each student.
    4. Every hostel resident will have to give the undertakings as mentioned in Appendix –G & H (P-48 & 49).
